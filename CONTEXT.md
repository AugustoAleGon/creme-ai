# Worker operations

Staff review seasonal workers' documents and determine whether each assignment can be booked into a consular appointment.

## Language

**Worker**: A person's persistent identity, independent of a season or job order.

**Job order**: A case-system-owned work opportunity referenced by its canonical external ID.

**Roster assignment**: One worker's participation in one job order, owned operationally by a capturist.

**Document version**: One immutable submission of a document type for an assignment. Review decisions are separate from its submitted content.

**Active document**: The most recent approved version of a document type, or the latest submission if none is approved.

**Correction task**: Outstanding work for the assignment's owning capturist after a document rejection or conflict.

**Readiness**: Satisfaction of employer approval, current-terms acceptance, required documents and independent QC approval.

**Appointment membership**: An assignment's booking in an appointment. Cancellation retains its historical record.
